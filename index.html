How buying works

Before buying: Please read the app details and installation requirements. Payments are handled by Payhip, not this website.

Choose your app and click Buy Now. You'll be taken to a secure Payhip checkout. After successful payment, Payhip provides a download link and sends purchase information by email. Keep your receipt and follow the app-specific instructions. If an app requires activation, its listing or download instructions will explain the process.

Our five paid apps are installable web apps (PWAs). Payhip provides a customer installation PDF, and your personal licence key arrives separately by email. Activate once online on up to two devices, then use the installed app offline.
