Are Airport Lounges Worth It? Questions to Ask

Airport lounges can offer a quieter place to wait, but value depends on the airport, the time of day and what is included.

Airport lounges can offer a quieter place to wait, but value depends on the airport, the time of day and what is included.

Check entry limits, opening hours, guest policies, food and drink options, and whether access is guaranteed at busy times.

Compare the lounge price with what you would otherwise spend in the terminal. Families should also check child admission rules.

If you book through an affiliate partner, review its terms and confirm your terminal before purchasing. An affiliate link does not change the need to verify details with the provider.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
