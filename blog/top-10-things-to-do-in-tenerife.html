Top 10 Things to do in Tenerife

Explore the top 10 things to do in Tenerife, Spain, for a mix of volcanic landscapes, historic towns, and outdoor adventures.

1. Explore Teide National Park and Mount Teide

• What to do: Take the cable car or hike up Mount Teide, Spain's highest peak at 3,718 meters, located inside a dramatic UNESCO World Heritage volcanic caldera.
• Highlight: See the rock formations at Roques de García, including Roque Cinchado.

2. Visit Masca Village and the Teno Mountains

• What to do: Drive through the winding mountain roads of the Teno Mountains to reach Masca Village, a traditional hamlet perched on dramatic ridge cliffs.
• Highlight: Dramatic deep valleys and panoramic hiking views down to the coast.

3. Go Whale and Dolphin Watching

• What to do: Board an eco-certified or small-group boat tour from ports like Puerto Colón or Los Gigantes.
• Highlight: Spot pilot whales and bottlenose dolphins in their natural year-round habitat off the coast.

4. Marvel at the Acantilados de Los Gigantes (Cliffs of the Giants)

• What to do: View the colossal sheer rock walls that rise up to 800 meters straight out of the Atlantic Ocean.
• Highlight: Combine a cliff viewing with a boat trip or a swim at the nearby black-sand beach.

5. Hike the Anaga Rural Park

• What to do: Walk through ancient misty laurel forests, dramatic ridge trails, and traditional cliffside settlements like Chinamada.
• Highlight: A cool, green microclimate contrasting sharply with the sunny southern coast.

6. Walk Through San Cristóbal de La Laguna

• What to do: Stour the pedestrianized, UNESCO-listed colonial old town with its colorful historic architecture and vibrant courtyards.
• Highlight: Historic churches, local markets like the Mercado Municipal, and a lively student atmosphere.

7. Swim in Natural Volcanic Rock Pools at Garachico

• What to do: Visit the historic coastal town of Garachico and swim in El Caletón, natural swimming pools formed by old lava flows.
• Highlight: Cobblestone streets, Plaza de la Libertad, and surrounding tidal pools.

8. See the Millenary Dragon Tree in Icod de los Vinos

• What to do: Visit Drago Milenario, a massive, ancient dragon tree said to be hundreds (or a thousand) of years old.
• Highlight: Explore the surrounding botanical park and local wine cellars.

9. Experience Family Fun at Siam Park or Loro Parque

• What to do: Spend a full day at Siam Park (a Thai-themed water park) or Loro Parque (a renowned zoo and conservation park).
• Highlight: World-class water slides and extensive animal habitats.

10. Sample Local Cuisine at a Guachiche

• What to do: Dine at traditional Guachinches—rustic, family-run pop-up eateries in the north of the island.
• Highlight: Try authentic papas arrugadas with mojo sauce, local cheeses, and regional Canarian wine.

Plan your trip logistics or check public transit routes via the TITSA Bus Network or explore tour options on TripAdvisor Tenerife.
