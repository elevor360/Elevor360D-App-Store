Travel Insurance Checklist Before You Book

Travel insurance is easier to assess when you compare the actual cover rather than only the headline price.

Travel insurance is easier to assess when you compare the actual cover rather than only the headline price.

Check medical cover, cancellation and curtailment limits, baggage cover, excesses and exclusions. Declare relevant medical conditions accurately as required by the insurer.

If you are cruising or taking part in activities, check whether additional cover is needed.

Read the policy wording and consider guidance from trusted consumer and government sources. This article is general information, not a recommendation of a particular policy.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
