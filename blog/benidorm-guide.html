Discover Benidorm: Beaches, Old Town and Holiday Ideas

Explore Benidorm, from its famous beaches to the historic old town.

Benidorm is a popular Costa Blanca holiday destination, known for its long sandy beaches and lively atmosphere.

Spend time on Levante Beach, explore the lanes of the old town and visit the Balcón del Mediterráneo viewpoint.

For a quieter day, consider Poniente Beach. Check opening times and booking conditions for attractions before you travel.
