Majorca for First-Time Visitors: Resorts and Day Trips

Majorca offers lively resorts, quieter coves, historic towns and mountain scenery. Choosing a base depends on whether you prefer beaches, nightlife or sightseeing.

Palma is a good option for architecture, cafés and exploring on foot. The old streets can be busy in peak season, so allow time rather than rushing between sights.

The Serra de Tramuntana area is known for scenic roads and villages. Check parking and transport before setting off, particularly in summer.

When comparing excursions, look at transfer times and whether tickets, meals or entrance fees are included. Keep a flexible day in your itinerary for rest.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
