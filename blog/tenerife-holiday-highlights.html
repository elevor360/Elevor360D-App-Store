Tenerife Holiday Guide: Beaches, Teide and Day Trips

Tenerife offers a mix of beach resorts, volcanic landscapes and traditional towns. The south is popular for sunshine-focused holidays, while the north has greener scenery and historic streets.

Tenerife offers a mix of beach resorts, volcanic landscapes and traditional towns. The south is popular for sunshine-focused holidays, while the north has greener scenery and historic streets.

Explore Teide National Park for volcanic scenery and viewpoints. Conditions at altitude can be cooler and windier than at the coast, so pack layers and check access arrangements.

For a relaxed day, choose a beach suited to your group and check local flag warnings before swimming. Coastal walks, markets and boat excursions are other options, depending on the season.

If you hire a car, allow time for mountain roads and parking. For organised trips, check what is included, the cancellation policy and whether pickup is near your accommodation.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
