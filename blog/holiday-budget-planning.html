How to Plan a Realistic Holiday Budget

A holiday budget works best when it includes costs that are easy to overlook, not just flights and accommodation.

A holiday budget works best when it includes costs that are easy to overlook, not just flights and accommodation.

List transport, baggage, meals, local taxes, excursions, insurance and spending money. Leave a contingency for unexpected expenses.

Compare the full cost of packages and separate bookings, including payment and cancellation terms.

Set a daily spending guide, but keep it flexible. A simple travel checklist or budget app can help you track plans before departure.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
