Málaga City Break: Culture, Beaches and Easy Planning

Málaga can work as both a short city break and a base for the Costa del Sol. Its historic centre, museums and waterfront are relatively close together.

Málaga can work as both a short city break and a base for the Costa del Sol. Its historic centre, museums and waterfront are relatively close together.

Plan a walk through the old centre and consider a visit to one or two museums rather than trying to fit everything into one day.

The waterfront and beaches offer a change of pace after sightseeing. In warmer months, take breaks in the shade and carry water.

Check transport from the airport and whether your accommodation is walkable from the areas you want to visit. Popular attractions may have timed-entry tickets.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
