Albufeira and the Algarve: Beaches, Old Town and Trips

Albufeira is a popular Algarve holiday base with beaches, restaurants and access to other coastal towns. The atmosphere varies between the old town and newer resort areas.

Spend time exploring the old town and nearby beaches. Coastal steps and slopes can be steep, so check access if mobility is a concern.

Boat trips and coastal sightseeing are popular, but routes depend on sea conditions. Confirm departure points, weather policies and any age restrictions.

If you want to explore beyond Albufeira, compare local buses, taxis and car hire. Booking accommodation close to your preferred area can reduce daily travel.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
