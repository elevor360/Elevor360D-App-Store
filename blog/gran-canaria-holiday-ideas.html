Gran Canaria: Beaches, Dunes and Mountain Villages

Gran Canaria combines southern beach resorts with dramatic inland landscapes. It is worth planning more than one type of day out to see the island beyond your hotel.

Gran Canaria combines southern beach resorts with dramatic inland landscapes. It is worth planning more than one type of day out to see the island beyond your hotel.

Maspalomas is known for its dunes and nearby coastline. Follow marked access routes in protected areas and take water and sun protection.

Inland villages and viewpoints offer a contrast to the coast. Mountain routes can be winding, and weather can change as you gain height.

Consider combining a relaxed beach day with one inland excursion. Compare travel times before booking and check accessibility if travelling with children or anyone with limited mobility.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
