Paphos Holiday Guide: History, Coast and Day Trips

Paphos combines coastal resorts with archaeological sites and opportunities to explore western Cyprus.

Paphos combines coastal resorts with archaeological sites and opportunities to explore western Cyprus.

Historic sites can involve considerable walking on uneven ground. Check opening hours, accessibility and sun protection before visiting.

The harbour area is a convenient starting point for a relaxed walk, cafés and views along the coast.

For longer excursions, compare guided tours with car hire and check how much travel time is involved. Keep some flexibility for weather and rest.

Before travelling, check current information from official providers and local authorities. Prices, availability and opening arrangements may change.
