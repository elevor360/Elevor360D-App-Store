Cancellation and refunds

UK consumers generally have a 14-day cancellation right for qualifying distance purchases. For digital content not supplied on a physical medium, the right can be lost when supply begins only if the legal requirements are met, including express consent to immediate supply, acknowledgement of the loss of the cancellation right and the required confirmation. A download alone does not automatically waive this right.

If digital content is faulty, not as described or otherwise fails to conform to the contract, customers retain their statutory rights and remedies, including repair, replacement or a refund/price reduction where required by law. We will investigate reported problems.
